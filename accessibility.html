Our Commitment to Accessibility

Our goal is to make this website easy to use and accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We strive to provide equal access to all visitors.

Web Users and Web Accessibility

Disability Description Common Barriers Accessibility Principles to Apply
Auditory Partial or complete loss of hearing. Videos without captions and audio without transcripts. Perceivable: Provide captions and transcripts.
Cognitive and Neurological Difficulty with memory, learning, reading, or processing information. Complicated instructions and confusing navigation. Understandable: Use simple language and consistent navigation.
Physical Limited movement, coordination, or muscle control. Small buttons and websites that require a mouse. Operable: Provide keyboard navigation and larger buttons.
Speech Difficulty producing clear or understandable speech. Websites that require voice communication. Operable: Provide text input and alternative communication methods.
Visual Blindness, low vision, or difficulty distinguishing colors. Missing alternative text, small text, and poor color contrast. Perceivable: Provide alternative text, readable fonts, and good contrast.
